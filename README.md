# Kleine Werkzeuge, die nichts von dir wollen

<p lang="en"><i>Small tools that want nothing from you.</i></p>

Ich bin Dennis, im Netz Dennis_mit_2n. Seit Sommer 2026 baue ich kleine Werkzeuge, die eine Sache gut machen: ohne Anmeldung, ohne Anzeigen, die meisten kostenlos und quelloffen. Programmieren hab ich mir selbst beigebracht, mit KI als Lernhilfe und Claude als Werkbank-Kollegen. Ich will verstehen, was ich baue – nicht nur bauen lassen. Die Namen sind fast alle von mir selbst ausgedacht – man merkt’s. 😉

<p lang="en">I’m Dennis, online as Dennis_mit_2n. Since summer 2026 I’ve been building small tools that do one thing well: no sign-up, no ads, most of them free and open source. I taught myself to code, with AI as my tutor and Claude as my workbench buddy. I want to understand what I build – not just have it built. I came up with almost all of the names myself – you can tell. 😉</p>

### The workbench

Languages so far: HTML, JavaScript, TypeScript, CSS, Python and PowerShell, plus a bit of Batch and Inno Setup.<br>Tools: VS Code, Git and GitHub, Claude Code, Electron.<br>Built on a Windows 11 laptop, tested on an Android phone and tablet with a stylus.

Suggestions for improvement are welcome – ideally as an issue in the repo in question.

<sub>To be honest: the browser tools count visits and clicks anonymously with GoatCounter – no cookies, never what you enter. Prismatical doesn’t count at all. Beyond that, Spectroton asks api.color.pizza for colour names, and Prismatical checks passwords with Have I Been Pwned only if you switch it on – even then only five characters of the hash leave your device, never the password. This page loads nothing from other servers: no counters, no stats, every image lives in this repo.</sub>
